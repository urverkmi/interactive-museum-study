# W1 — Joachim Beuckelaer, Brothel Scene, 1563

## Version A (museum's original text, word for word)

People who are obviously drunk have gathered around a table and are behaving very badly indeed. The man is fondling two women, and a third woman is staring so deep into her tankard that she is forgetting to feed her child. The male customer is a better class of person. He is more smartly dressed, and is drinking wine poured into his glass from a pewter flagon. The women, on the other hand, are simply dressed and are drinking beer from earthenware jugs. The kitchenmaid on the left is hard at work, and seems detached from the goings-on around the table. She is looking out at the viewer with a dubious look on her face. At the very back there is an old man taking his ease by a fire in the hearth. A third man is just emerging from the room at the back. He is the one who identifies the setting as a brothel and not a kitchen or inn as art historians used to think.

## Version B/C segments

### Around the table (R1)

A group of people sit around a table. They are clearly drunk and behaving badly. One man has his hands on two women. Another woman stares into her beer mug. She forgets to feed her child. The man is from a higher class than the women. His clothes are smarter. He drinks wine, poured from a pewter jug. The women wear simple clothes. They drink beer from clay jugs.

### The maid on the left (R2)

On the left, a kitchen maid is busy working. She seems to have no part in what happens at the table. She looks straight out at us. Her face shows doubt.

### At the back of the room (R3)

At the far back, an old man rests by the fire. Another man is coming out of a back room. This man tells us what kind of place this is: a brothel. Art historians used to think the scene showed a kitchen or an inn.
